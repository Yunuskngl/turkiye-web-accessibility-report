How easy are Türkiye's most visited websites to use for people with disabilities?

We automatically checked the home pages of 878 popular websites in Türkiye. We looked for problems that make a site hard to use for people who, for example, use a screen reader, cannot use a mouse or have low vision. Checked on 11 November 2026.

The results at a glance

  • 95.4% of home pages had at least one problem on a computer screen
  • 58.8 problems per home page on average, on a computer screen
  • 95.1% of home pages had at least one problem on a phone screen
  • 55.3 problems per home page on average, on a phone screen

What counts as a problem? One part of a page that fails one automatic check, for example one image without a text description. Automatic checks find only some of the barriers people meet, so a page with no problems found is not automatically easy for everyone to use.

About this report

The report shows results by sector only. It does not rank or name websites, and it is not a legal audit. The numbers come from one tool (axe-core) and cannot be compared directly with reports that use other tools.

Want to see the result for one website? Use Check a website. How we chose and checked the sites is explained on How we did it.
