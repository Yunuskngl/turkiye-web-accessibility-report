Accessibility of Türkiye's most visited home pages

An automated check of 40 home pages of popular websites in Türkiye for WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA failures that software can detect. Checked on 11 November 2026 with axe-core 4.14.0.

Pilot results. These numbers come from a small pilot run used to test the method. They are not the final report.

Key numbers

  • 95.0% of home pages had at least one detected failure (desktop view)
  • 48.4 detected failures per home page on average (desktop view)
  • 95.0% of home pages had at least one detected failure (mobile view)
  • 39.1 detected failures per home page on average (mobile view)

A detected failure is one element on a page that fails one automated WCAG check, for example one image without alternative text. Automated checks find only part of the barriers people meet, so a page with no detected failures is not necessarily accessible.

About this report

This report shows sector-level results only. It does not rank websites and it is not a legal compliance audit. Our numbers come from the axe-core engine and are not directly comparable with reports that use other tools, such as the WebAIM Million.
